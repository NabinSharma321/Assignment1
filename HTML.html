Welcome to Pokhara

Pokhara is one of the most beautiful cities in Nepal. It is famous for its peaceful lakes, beautiful mountains, and adventure activities. Tourists from around the world visit Pokhara to enjoy the view of the Annapurna mountain range and Phewa Lake.

Popular Attractions

  • Phewa Lake
  • World Peace Pagoda
  • Davis Falls
  • Gupteshwor Cave
  • Sarangkot

Pokhara is also known as the tourism capital of Nepal because it offers activities such as boating, paragliding, trekking, and sightseeing.
